agentready-bot

A slow, polite crawler that reads the public pages of Telangana government department and district websites.

This is an independent project. It is not run by, or affiliated with, the Government of Telangana or any of its departments.

What it does

agentready-bot reads pages and documents that any visitor can open without logging in. We use them to:

  • produce an accessibility scorecard for each site (GIGW 3.0 and WCAG 2.1 level AA), with a list of suggested fixes;
  • check how easily AI assistants can read and cite the site, for example whether PDFs have a text layer and whether notices carry dates;
  • offer an unofficial, accessible reading view in Telugu, English and Urdu. It always links back to the official page.

Every piece of text we keep is stored with its official source address and the date we read it.

How to recognise it

Every request sends this User-Agent header, where <version> is the software version and the link points to this page:

agentready-bot/<version> (+https://this-page-address/)

The robots.txt name to use is agentready-bot.

The rules it follows

Crawler behaviour
RuleWhat it means for your server
robots.txtRead before anything else and obeyed, including Crawl-delay. If robots.txt returns a server error or doesn't respond, the bot fetches nothing from your site in that run.
SpeedOne request at a time, at most one every 5 seconds per website. A longer Crawl-delay in robots.txt is honoured.
HoursNormally runs only overnight, between 22:00 and 06:00 India Standard Time. Occasional manual checks outside those hours keep the same 5-second limit.
VolumeAt most 500 pages per website per run, and no file larger than 50 MB.
Repeat visitsUses If-None-Match and If-Modified-Since, so unchanged files cost you a short 304 Not Modified reply instead of a full download.
ScopeStays on the website being checked. Fetches web pages, PDFs and office documents only; skips images, scripts, archives and video.
RetriesAfter a server error or timeout it waits (10 seconds, then 20) and tries at most twice more. It honours Retry-After.
NeverNever submits forms, logs in, solves CAPTCHAs, or tries to get around any access control. Never turns off certificate checks: a certificate problem is recorded as a finding, and nothing from that site is fetched.

How to slow it down or block it

Add rules to your site's /robots.txt. The bot reads robots.txt again at the start of every run.

Block it completely:

User-agent: agentready-bot
Disallow: /

Keep it out of some folders:

User-agent: agentready-bot
Disallow: /admin/
Disallow: /internal/

Ask it to wait longer between requests (seconds):

User-agent: agentready-bot
Crawl-delay: 30

If you would rather not edit robots.txt, write to us (below) and we will stop crawling your site by hand.
